# Citing Brian2 Atlas

For Atlas results, cite the manuscript by Xinjun Li,
“A Unified Intermediate Representation and Execution Architecture for Heterogeneous and Distributed Neural Simulation,”
in the [preprint repository](https://github.com/RockLi/brian2-atlas-preprint),
and record the exact [Atlas source commit](https://github.com/RockLi/brian2-atlas)
used for the experiment. Use the publication metadata when a released paper is available.

Atlas retains the Brian2 modelling frontend; please also cite its original article:

Stimberg, M, Brette, R, Goodman, DFM.
“Brian 2, an Intuitive and Efficient Neural Simulator.”
eLife 8 (2019): e47314.
doi: [10.7554/eLife.47314](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.47314).
